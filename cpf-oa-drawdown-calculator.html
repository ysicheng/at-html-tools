Important CPF treatment

  • Extra interest is not extra OA drawdown yield. For members 55 and above, CPF currently pays extra interest on the first S$30,000 of combined CPF balances and another 1% on the next S$30,000, subject to an OA cap of S$20,000. The extra interest earned on OA goes to the RA (or SA where applicable), not back into OA. It is therefore excluded from this OA-only drawdown calculation.
  • This is not an annuity or CPF LIFE estimate. It is a fixed-term depletion illustration: it deliberately reaches zero after the selected term. CPF LIFE is designed to pay for life and works differently.
  • Timing convention: to match a conservative end-of-month withdrawal assumption, the model deducts the draw first each month, accrues that month’s interest on the remaining month-end balance, and credits the accumulated interest each December. CPF Board’s operational calculation and your actual withdrawal date/settings prevail.
  • Private by design: calculations happen only in your browser. This page has no account login, analytics, server storage or data upload.
